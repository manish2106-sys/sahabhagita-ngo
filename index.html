Who We Are

Two Decades of
Grassroots Impact

Sahabhagita Shahri & Gramin Vikas Samiti began its journey in 2004, founded by a group of development practitioners, educators, and field professionals in Madhya Pradesh who shared a common belief: sustainable development is built through community participation, local ownership, and practical solutions that endure beyond the life of a project.

The name Sahabhagita means partnership, shared responsibility, and collective action. For more than two decades, we have maintained a strong field-first approach — working directly with underserved communities, local institutions, government bodies, and industry partners across Sidhi, Singrauli, and beyond.

Our grassroots work includes establishing community learning centres for out-of-school children, enabling 350+ rural women to develop small-scale mushroom-based livelihoods, and supporting municipal bodies in implementing decentralized waste management systems under the Swachh Bharat Mission.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our STEM education, women empowerment, smart agriculture, and waste management initiatives.

How does Sahabhagita advance STEM education through Project Pratham Pratibha?

Project Pratham Pratibha installs fully equipped physical innovation labs in partner government secondary schools across Madhya Pradesh and NCR. Rather than relying on blackboard theory, 15 trained field mentors guide 10,000 learners through a structured 14-phase 'White Box' curriculum. Students directly trace voltages, build circuits, program microcontrollers, and assemble connected sensors — with at least 60% female student enrollment.

What women empowerment and livelihood programs does Sahabhagita operate?

Sahabhagita has organized and nurtured 32 Women Self-Help Groups (SHGs) in Singrauli, enabling 350+ rural and tribal women to launch small-scale mushroom cultivation and agro-processing businesses. The program provides training, spawn supply, bio-hygiene protocol, and direct market linkage, creating independent household income streams.

How is Sahabhagita advancing smart agriculture and rural development?

Our rural development model combines practical agricultural science with community ownership. We train marginal farmers in Singrauli and Sidhi on organic bio-composting, water conservation, solar irrigation pumps, and crop diversification. By eliminating expensive chemical inputs and linking harvests directly to regional vendors, farming families secure durable self-reliance.

What is Sahabhagita's role in municipal waste management under Swachh Bharat Mission?

Sahabhagita has served as technical implementation and community mobilization partner for municipal corporations including Maihar and Singrauli. We implement decentralized solid waste segregation, community-level organic composting, and electronic waste (e-waste) collection, diverting tons of waste from open dumps while repurposing discarded electronics into educational components.

Are donations to Sahabhagita eligible for 80G tax deductions?

Yes. Sahabhagita holds perpetual tax exemption status under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (Registration: AACAS8949KF20214) as well as Section 12A (ITBA/EXM/S/12A/2021-22/1036077583(1)). Every donor receives an instant computer-generated 80G Tax Exemption Certificate with the NGO's official seal and PAN (AACAS8949K).

How can corporate companies partner with Sahabhagita for CSR projects?

Sahabhagita is registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs under MCA CSR-1 (Registration: CSR00094897, SRN: AB5710340) and verified on NITI Aayog's NGO Darpan (MP/2025/0702269). Past corporate partners include Reliance Power (Sasan UMPP). We provide transparent milestone-based execution, quarterly CSR audits, and audited Utilization Certificates (UC) aligned with Schedule VII Item (ii) and Item (iv).
